NOUGHTPAD DOCS

Launch a token on Robinhood Chain and route its trading fees into rewards for holders on another network. This beta runs every step on a shared server with play ETH and live market prices

01 · overviewA launchpad that pays holders

Noughtpad launches tokens on a bonding curve. Every trade pays a 3% fee: 1% to the protocol and 2% into the token’s own vault. A reward service empties the vault in rounds, bridges it to the network the creator picked, buys the chosen asset and pays holders by their share of supply.

This is a play-money beta. There is one shared launchpad on a server: every visitor sees the same tokens, trades, vaults and rounds. Balances are play ETH with no value. Reward amounts are converted at live market prices from CoinGecko. The $NOUGHT token is real and separate.

02 · play ETHA key in this browser

The first visit creates a random 32-character key in this browser. The server derives a public wallet id from it with SHA-256 and gives that wallet 10 play ETH. Clearing site data loses the key and the wallet with it.

10 play ETH
Faucet
+5 play ETH once every 24 hours
Shown to others

04 · pricingConstant product with virtual reserves

Each token starts with 4 virtual ETH against 1,073,000,000 virtual tokens. Price is ETH reserve divided by token reserve, and every buy moves along x × y = k. 793,100,000 tokens can be bought from the curve; the rest of the 1,000,000,000 supply stays with it.

├── supply                 1,000,000,000
├── sold on the curve      up to 793,100,000
├── starting market cap    about 3.73 ETH
├── buy                    tokens out = token reserve × ETH in after fee ÷ (ETH reserve + ETH in after fee)
├── sell                   ETH out = ETH reserve × tokens in ÷ (token reserve + tokens in), minus 3%
├── curve complete         after about 11.7 ETH of buys; then only sells are possible

06 · serviceCollect, bridge, buy, pay

The service looks at every vault at most once every 30 seconds, whenever the token is opened, traded or listed. A round starts when both conditions hold.

├── vault       at least 0.004 ETH
├── cooldown    3 minutes since the previous round
├── collect     0 s · the vault is emptied
├── bridge      8 s · routed to the reward network
├── buy         22 s · converted at the market price of that moment
├── paid        34 s · the payout lands in holder wallets

07 · snapshotShare of supply, fixed at the round

The round uses a snapshot of balances taken when it starts. A wallet holding 1% of total supply receives 1% of the bought amount. Tokens still on the curve are not a holder, so their share stays in the vault as retained reward and other holders are not topped up. Buying after the snapshot counts from the next round.

Payout
round amount × wallet balance ÷ 1,000,000,000
Retained
round amount − all payouts
Estimate on the page
current vault after the 0.5% cost, at current prices

08 · infrastructureOne server, one state

Every action goes to a Vercel Function that loads the token and the wallet from Upstash Redis, takes short locks, runs the engine and writes the result back. The page polls every 4 seconds and computes quotes with the same engine file the server uses.

Rate limit
40 actions per 10 seconds per IP
History kept
last 400 trades and 30 rounds per token, last 100 rewards per wallet
Prices
cached for 2 minutes; last known prices if CoinGecko does not answer

11 · questionsQuestions

Is the money real?No. Play ETH and the rewards it buys have no value. Only $NOUGHT on Pons is real.
Why did I get nothing from a round?You had no balance when the snapshot was taken, or your share rounded to zero.
Why is most of the round kept?Early on most of the supply is still on the curve, and the curve’s share stays in the vault.
When is the next round?The token page shows the vault against the 0.004 ETH threshold, the cooldown and the next check.
I cleared my browser. Where is my wallet?The key was only in that browser. A new key starts a new wallet.
