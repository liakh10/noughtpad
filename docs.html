NOUGHTPAD DOCS

Launch a token on Pons, Robinhood Chain, and route its creator tax into rewards for holders on BNB Chain, Base, Ethereum or Hyperliquid. Every step is a public transaction

01 · overviewA launchpad that pays holders

Noughtpad launches regular Pons V2 tokens on Robinhood Chain. Each launch sets a 2% creator tax paid to the Noughtpad operator. A reward service sweeps that tax, sends it through Relay to the network the creator picked, buys the chosen asset and pays holders by their share of supply.

02 · connectYour wallet, your tokens

Connect any injected wallet such as MetaMask, Rabby or Coinbase Wallet. Noughtpad adds Robinhood Chain to the wallet if it is missing. Buys and sells are sent from your wallet to the curve; the page simulates each trade first and sets 3% slippage protection.

03 · one paymentPay once, go live on Pons

Pick a name, ticker, image, description, X link and the reward route. You then send one payment to the Noughtpad operator. The server checks the payment on chain, launches the token from the operator with the 2% creator tax, and runs your first buy with the tokens sent to your wallet.

├── Pons launch fee           0.0005 ETH, read from the factory
├── launch gas                0.0006 ETH flat
├── first buy                 optional, any amount on top
├── image                     cropped to 256 px and served from noughtpad
├── if the launch fails       the payment is sent back minus transfer gas
├── if the first buy fails    the buy amount is sent back

Why the operator launches: Pons only lets the deployer of a token or its own operator sweep curve fees. Launching from the Noughtpad operator is what lets the service collect the tax without asking you to sign every few minutes.

05 · serviceSweep, bridge, pay

The service runs every five minutes. Each run advances every token by one step.

├── sweep       fees above 0.0002 ETH are swept from the curve into the Pons fee escrow and claimed; the escrow balance change is credited to that token
├── round       starts when the token vault holds 0.01 ETH and 10 minutes passed since its last round
├── snapshot    balances from Transfer logs at the round block
├── deposit     one Relay deposit on Robinhood Chain pays the bridge, the swap and the payout
├── payout      Disperse on BNB Chain, Base and Ethereum; token transfers for USDC on Hyperliquid; HYPE is forwarded by the operator
├── settle      the round is marked paid when Relay reports success

06 · snapshotShare of supply

A wallet holding 1% of the 1,000,000,000 supply receives 1% of the reward bought in the round. Contracts are not paid: the Pons curve, Uniswap pools, the fee escrow and any other address with code keep their share, and it is not redistributed. Up to 150 of the largest holders are paid per round, 40 for USDC on Hyperliquid.

09 · questionsQuestions

Does my token show on Pons?Yes. It is a normal Pons V2 launch and trades there too.
Why did I get nothing from a round?You held nothing at the snapshot, your wallet is a contract, or your share rounded to zero.
When does the first round run?When the vault reaches 0.01 ETH, which needs about 0.5 ETH of trading volume.
Can the reward route change later?No. It is fixed at launch.
What happens after graduation?Trading moves to Uniswap v4 on Pons. The curve tax stops; pool fees are handled by Pons.
